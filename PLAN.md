# Move the "watch the story" tile to the top of a circle's page

Right now, when you open a circle, the "watch the story" tile sits near the bottom of the page — below the Today/Overall toggle, the task list, and everyone's progress — and it only shows up in goal-sharing ("parallel") circles. This moves it up and makes it consistent everywhere.

**What changes**

- The "watch the story" tile becomes the **first thing you see** inside a circle, right beneath the header (above the Today/Overall toggle and the task list).
- It will now appear in **all circles** — both the goal-sharing kind and the shared-goal ("collective") kind — not just one type.
- It still only appears **when there's actually a story to watch that day** (someone has shared a moment). If no one has posted, the tile stays hidden, just like today.
- Tapping it opens the group story player exactly as it does now. 

**What stays the same**

- The tile's look, wording, and the story player itself are unchanged.
- The rest of the page — the Today/Overall toggle, the task list, the "add your mile" prompt, and everyone's progress — keeps its current order; only the story tile relocates to the top.
